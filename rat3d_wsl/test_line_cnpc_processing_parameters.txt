processing steps of the test line

1.	Define and check  the  geometry of the line.
2.	Calculate and apply the elevation statics (floation datum :100m ,replacement velocity : 2500 m/s
3.	Surface wave attenuation 
4.	Linear noise attenuation (apparent velocity of linear noise: 100-2500 m/s)
5.	Wild amplitude attenuation.
6.	Predict deconvolution  .  (operator length:200ms , time window1, 100-1500 ms , predictive distance :36ms.  time window 2 ,1500-3000ms, predictive distance :42ms).
7.	Velocity analysis (every 20 cmps).
8.	Calculate and apply the residual statics.
9.	Velocity analysis (every 20 cmps).
10.	Stack.
11.	Random noise attenuation.
12.	SegY output.
